We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information, and we have not done so in the last 12 months. We also do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as California defines that term. We do not use it for targeted advertising or for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

We have never knowingly sold or shared the personal information of anyone under 16.

Where your data goes

We are based in the United States and our service providers are largely based there, so your information will be transferred to and stored in the US.

If you are in the EEA or UK, that is a country whose data protection laws differ from your own. Where we transfer your data out of the EEA or UK we rely on appropriate safeguards — normally the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we rely on.

How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe, and then a minimal suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake.
  • Inactive subscribers: reviewed periodically; if you have not opened anything in about three years we will re-confirm or remove you.
  • Contact form messages: up to [[ e.g. 24 months ]] after the conversation ends, longer if it concerns a booking or a legal matter.
  • Server and analytics logs: typically 12 months or less.
  • Consent records: for as long as we rely on the consent, and for a period afterwards to show we handled it properly.

When we no longer need something, we delete it or strip it of anything identifying.

How we protect it

The site is served over an encrypted connection, form submissions are encrypted in transit, and access to our contact records is limited to people who need it and protected by strong authentication.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach ever affects your personal data and puts you at risk, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Your rights (EEA & UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectification — have anything inaccurate corrected;
  • Erasure — have your data deleted, in the circumstances the law provides;
  • Restriction — have us pause using it while something is being sorted out;
  • Portability — receive it in a machine-readable format, or have it sent elsewhere;
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time, absolutely;
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it;
  • Complain — to your local data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Your rights (United States)

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Florida, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky or Rhode Island — or any other state with a comprehensive privacy law in force — you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it and who we disclosed it to;
  • Access a copy of it, in a portable form;
  • Correct anything inaccurate;
  • Delete it, subject to the exceptions the law allows;
  • Opt out of any sale, of sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, and of targeted advertising and profiling — we do none of these, but the right stands;
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect any;
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We will not give you a worse service, a worse price, or cut off your email because you asked.

Appeals. Several states give you the right to appeal if we refuse a request. If we turn you down, we will tell you why and how to appeal. Reply to that message and a different person will review it, normally within 45 days. If we reject your appeal you may complain to your state Attorney General.

You may use an authorised agent to make a request for you. We will ask for proof that you authorised them, and we may still need to verify your identity directly.

How to exercise your rights

Email Orderofthecrowofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy request" and tell us what you want. For marketing email, the unsubscribe link in any email is the fastest route.

To protect you, we will ask you to confirm your identity — usually by replying from the email address we hold. We will not ask for a copy of your ID for a simple request.

We answer within 30 days (EEA/UK) or 45 days (US states), and will tell you if we need longer. It is free, unless a request is repetitive or excessive.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you must be at least 16 to join the mailing list, or have your parent or guardian's consent.

If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it promptly.
