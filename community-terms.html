What this is

The Cult of the Crow is the private community run by Christopher Mark Robers Kreutz, trading as Order of the Crow. These terms govern your membership of it.

They sit on top of our main Terms & Conditions, our Privacy Policy and our Code of Conduct, all of which still apply. Where these community terms and the main terms disagree about something inside the community, these ones win.

The community is hosted on GoHighLevel (HighLevel Inc.). That platform has its own terms and privacy policy, and by using the community you are also using their service.

Joining

You join by filling in the join form and being granted access. There's no approval queue and no waiting list at the moment — but we can refuse or withdraw membership at our discretion, and we don't have to give a reason.

Membership is personal to you. It isn't a purchase, it isn't property, and it isn't something you can sell, lend or transfer.

Who can join

You must be at least 18 years old to join. This is an adults-only space: conversation happens in real time and is not pre-moderated, and we are not set up to look after minors.

If we learn that someone under 18 has joined, we will remove them and delete their information.

It's free right now

Membership currently costs nothing. There is no subscription, no card on file, and nothing to cancel.

We may introduce paid membership or paid tiers in future. If we do, it will not be applied retrospectively to you without your active agreement — you would be told first, told the price and the billing period, and asked to opt in. Nobody gets charged for something they already joined for free.

Because there is nothing to pay, there is nothing to refund. If that changes, this clause and a refund policy get rewritten before any payment is taken.

Your access is yours alone

Don't share your login. Don't let someone else use your account. Don't post an invite link somewhere public.

You're responsible for what happens under your account. If you think someone else has got into it, tell us.

What's inside stays inside

This is the important one, and it's the one that gets people removed.

Everything posted in the community by us — unreleased tracks, demos, alternate takes, uncut video, artwork, photos, written material, anything at all — is members-only and confidential. It is shared with you on the condition that you keep it in the room.

You may not:

  • download, record, screenshot, screen-capture or rip members-only material for sharing;
  • repost, reupload or distribute it anywhere — social media, streaming, messaging apps, forums, file-sharing, anywhere;
  • describe unreleased material in public in enough detail to spoil it;
  • use any of it commercially, or to train or develop any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model;
  • pass it to anyone who isn't a member, including privately.

You keep no rights in that material. Nothing shared with you in the community transfers any ownership or licence to you beyond viewing and listening to it yourself, as a member, while you are a member.

Leaking members-only material is grounds for immediate and permanent removal without warning, and where a leak causes real harm we reserve every legal remedy available to us.

What you post

You keep ownership of anything you post. But by posting it you give us permission to display it, store it, and moderate it within the community, and to quote it back to you in support conversations.

You're responsible for what you post. Only post things you have the right to share, and don't post anything unlawful, or anything that would breach someone else's copyright or privacy.

Don't post unsolicited demos, songs or lyrics. Same position as the main terms: we don't accept them, we can't promise to keep them confidential, and if we later release something that resembles material you posted unasked, we owe you nothing for it.

Other members can see what you post. Treat everything you write here as visible to the whole room, because it is.

How you behave

Our Code of Conduct is part of these terms. Read it — it's short, and it's the practical version of this section.

The short version: look after each other, don't harass anybody, don't spam, and don't take what's in the room outside it.

Moderation and removal

We moderate this space. That means we can remove posts, edit or hide content, mute an account, suspend someone temporarily, or remove them permanently.

Ordinarily we'd warn you first and explain the problem. For serious things — leaking members-only material, harassment, threats, illegal content — we will act immediately and without warning.

If you think we've got it wrong, email us and say so. A real person will look at it again. We don't promise to change our mind, but we do promise to actually read it.

We aren't obliged to monitor everything, and we're not responsible for what other members post. If you see something wrong, report it.

What we don't promise

This is a community, not a service you've bought. Specifically, we don't promise:

  • that it will always be available, or free of faults or interruptions;
  • that any particular content will appear, or appear on any schedule;
  • that any perk described anywhere — on the website, in an email, in the community itself — will continue to be offered;
  • that the community will continue to exist at all.

We may change what the community includes, move it to a different platform, or close it entirely. If we close it, we'll give you reasonable notice where we can.

The disclaimers and limits of liability in our main Terms & Conditions apply here in full.

Your privacy

What we collect and why is set out in the Privacy Policy. A few things specific to the community:

  • Your display name and anything you post are visible to other members. Don't use your full legal name if you'd rather not.
  • Other members can read what you write. We can't control what they do with it, so don't post anything here you'd be upset to see elsewhere.
  • The hosting platform will also process your data under its own policy.
  • Don't share other members' personal information, screenshots of their posts, or anything they've said in here, outside the community. That's covered in the Code of Conduct too.

Leaving

You can leave whenever you like, and you don't have to explain. Email us and we'll remove your membership.

Leaving the community doesn't automatically unsubscribe you from the mailing list — those are separate. Use the unsubscribe link in any email for that, or ask us to do both.

The obligation to keep members-only material confidential continues after you leave. So does anything else here that by its nature should survive.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms — most likely when the community grows, moves platform, or gains paid tiers. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current version.

For material changes we'll tell you inside the community and by email. If you don't agree with a change, your remedy is to leave.

Which law applies

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Washington, United States, and the dispute-resolution provisions in our main Terms & Conditions apply here too — talk to us first, then the courts of King County, Washington.

If you're a consumer in the EEA or the UK, this doesn't remove the mandatory protections of the country you live in.
