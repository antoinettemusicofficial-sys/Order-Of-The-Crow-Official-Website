02 / THE CULT OF THE CROW

YOU ARE NOT ALONE

Order of the Crow is for people who feel outside of normal society. The ones who never fit the room they were put in, and stopped pretending they did.

The imagery is cult imagery on purpose. The writing gets cryptic on purpose. If you've ever gone hunting for the meaning underneath a record — the clue behind the lyric, the thing the artwork won't say out loud — you already know why.

Strip it back and that's what this is: a hub of weirdos, uniting. Let's rise together.
