The spirit of this place

Order of the Crow has always been for people who feel outside of it. Outcasts. People who never quite fit the shape they were handed.

That's the whole point of this room, and it's the reason this page exists. A space built for people who've been treated badly elsewhere has to be a place where that doesn't happen again. Everything below is downstream of that one idea.

This isn't a long list of rules for their own sake. It's here so that when something goes wrong, everyone already knows how it gets handled — and so moderation looks consistent rather than like somebody's mood on the day.

What's expected of you

  • Look after each other. Outcasts don't do to other people what was done to them.
  • Assume good faith. Text strips out tone. Read the generous version first, and ask before you take offence.
  • Disagree without contempt. Different taste is welcome — in music, in anything. Sneering at someone for what they like is not.
  • Let people be new. Someone who just found the band is as welcome as someone who's been here since the first single.
  • Let people be quiet. Plenty of members will read for months before posting. That's a completely normal way to be here.
  • Keep what's shared here, here. Section 04 — it's the one that matters most.

What isn't tolerated

None of this is a grey area:

  • Harassment of any kind — following someone around the community, repeated unwanted contact, sexual harassment, pile-ons.
  • Hate. Nothing targeting anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, gender, gender identity or sexual orientation. Not as a joke, not ironically, not "just asking questions."
  • Threats or incitement to violence, against anyone, including yourself.
  • Doxxing — posting anyone's real name, address, workplace, phone number or other private information without their clear permission.
  • Sexual content of any kind. No explicit images, video, links or writing, and no sexual comments aimed at anyone here. It doesn't matter who is in it, who made it, or whether everyone involved agreed to it — this isn't that kind of room. It comes down, and posting it again gets you removed.
  • Anything sexual involving a minor, in any form — photographed, drawn, written or AI-generated. This is removed immediately, means permanent removal without warning, and is reported to law enforcement. There is no version of this we discuss rather than report.
  • Gore and shock content. Real violence, injury, death, or anything posted to disturb people rather than to say something.
  • Encouraging or glorifying self-harm, suicide or eating disorders, and graphic descriptions of any of them. Talking honestly about your own mental health is welcome here and always will be — see below for where the line is.
  • Spam and self-promotion. Talking about your own music in conversation is fine. Dropping links and leaving is not.
  • Scams, phishing, malware, or trying to sell anything to other members.
  • Impersonating Chris, anyone connected to the band, a moderator, or another member.
  • Leaks and piracy — of our material or anyone else's.

If you're about to post something and you're weighing whether it fits here: that hesitation is usually the answer.

Where the line is on mental health

This band's records are about the fragile line between enlightenment and madness, and this room was built for people who feel outside of everything. So people are going to talk about hard things here, and they should be able to.

Saying you're struggling is always allowed. What isn't is content that romanticises self-destruction, gives instructions, or is graphic enough to hurt somebody else reading it.

If someone here seems to be in real danger, don't try to carry it alone and don't handle it in public — tell a moderator. And if that person is you, please contact your local crisis or emergency service. We're a music community, not a support service, and we would much rather you were looked after properly than that we got it wrong.

What's in the room stays in the room

This is the rule that the whole community depends on, so it gets said plainly.

Unreleased tracks, demos, alternate takes, uncut video, artwork, anything posted here before it's public — none of it leaves. No reuploading, no reposting, no ripping, no sharing the file with one trusted friend, no describing an unreleased song in enough detail to spoil it.

The reason is simple: the only way Chris can share work this early is if it stays in a room he trusts. One leak and everybody loses the thing that makes this worth joining.

Leaking gets you removed permanently, without a warning first. The full legal position is in the Community Terms.

Other people's privacy

People say things in here they wouldn't say publicly. That only keeps working if it's respected.

  • Don't screenshot other members' posts and put them elsewhere.
  • Don't repeat what someone said in here outside of here, especially if it identifies them.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information, even if they told you themselves.
  • If someone shares something difficult, don't make it a topic elsewhere.

Also worth remembering for your own sake: other members can see what you post, and we can't control what they do with it. Post accordingly.

Reporting something

If you see something that breaks this — or something that just feels wrong — tell us. Use the platform's report function if it has one, or email Orderofthecrowofficial@gmail.com.

Useful things to include: what happened, where, roughly when, and a screenshot if you have one. But report it even if you have none of that.

We won't tell the other person who reported them. Reporting someone in good faith will never be held against you, even if we end up deciding no rule was broken.

If you're the one being harassed, you don't have to engage with it first. Report it and stop replying — that's the right move, not a failure to handle it yourself.

What happens when someone breaks this

Usually in this order, depending on how bad it is and whether it's happened before:

  1. A quiet word. Most things stop here. People misjudge a room; being told is often enough.
  2. The post comes down, with an explanation of why.
  3. A formal warning. On record, so a pattern is visible if it continues.
  4. A temporary mute or suspension, so things can cool off.
  5. Permanent removal.

Some things skip straight to the end. Leaking members-only material, threats, hate, doxxing, gore posted to disturb people, and scams all mean immediate permanent removal with no warning.

Anything sexual involving a minor is removed and reported to law enforcement, without exception and without a conversation about it first.

Other sexual content isn't in that category, but it still doesn't belong here: the post comes down, and posting it again gets you removed.

We'd rather explain than punish. But this room existing at all depends on it being safe, and that comes first.

If you think we got it wrong

Email Orderofthecrowofficial@gmail.com and tell us why. A person will read it properly and look at the decision again.

We won't always change our mind, and we won't discuss another member's situation with you. But you'll get an answer, and it won't be automated.
