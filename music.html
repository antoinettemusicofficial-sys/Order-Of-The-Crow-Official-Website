02 / THE CONCEPT

A record built on raw contrast

At its core, Dead Man is a reflection on the duality of the human condition. As people, we are constantly walking a razor-thin path between the light and the dark.

The tracks delve into the chaotic, often disorienting process of a soul awakening — a transformative journey where finding your true self sometimes means losing your mind along the way.

It is a record designed to mirror the turbulent experience of spiritual and emotional evolution.

  • SOUL AWAKENING

03 / THE SYMBOLISM

READ THE COVER

The struggle is encapsulated in the album's visual identity. Two crows — one stark white, the other pitch black — stand watch on either side of a massive black hole. These sentinels represent the opposing forces of our nature, while the black hole itself serves as the ultimate portal between light and darkness.

It is the inescapable void we all must cross to find balance.
